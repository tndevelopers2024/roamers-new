(01) The idea

You don’t need a travel group. You just need the courage to go. We’ll bring the people.

Roamers is a social travel club based in Chennai. We run small-group trips across India and beyond, built for everyone who is done waiting for their friends’ calendars to line up. Come alone or bring a friend — either way, you won’t be alone for long.

  1. 01

    Everything included

    Transport, stays, meals, permits and the plan. One booking. Zero spreadsheets.

  2. 02

    Led by trip captains

    Leaders and local guides who know the shortcut, the sunset spot and the good chai.

  3. 03

    Off the brochure

    Hand-picked food, stays and places you won’t find on a tour-bus itinerary.

(02) How it works

How strangers
become a squad.

It takes about a week. Here’s the play-by-play.

Day 01Just you.

  1. Day 01

    Choose a trip.

    Pick a place, a date, a vibe. Book one seat — really, just one. Then you’re added to a group chat full of people you haven’t met yet.

  2. Day 02

    Meet your people.

    Awkward hellos at the pickup point. Snacks get shared by the first chai stop, and by dinner there’s a running joke. Trip captains are suspiciously good at icebreakers.

  3. Day 03

    Explore together.

    Summits, backwaters, night markets, stargazing at 4,000 metres. The plan is handled, so all you do is show up for the moments — with people as curious as you are.

  4. Day 04

    Come back with stories.

    And a camera roll you’ll scroll for months, a reunion already on the calendar, and a group chat that refuses to go quiet.
